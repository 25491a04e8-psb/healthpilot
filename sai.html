🛡️ Safety & Privacy

Responsible AI

  • No automated diagnosis.
  • No medicine prescriptions.
  • Demonstration results are labelled.
  • Medical decisions require professional review.

Emergency support

For severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, stroke symptoms or loss of consciousness, seek emergency care immediately.

In India, call 112 for emergency assistance where available. Do not wait for an AI response.

Privacy information

This standalone page does not send reports to an AI server. A production system requires secure storage, consent, access controls and privacy safeguards.
